RFQ

Copy this to every supplier

Need 1600×3200×20mm sintered stone — whole body with vein throughout thickness. Please confirm: 1) Is pigment mixed into the raw material body, or only a printed/glazed surface layer? 2) Do veins continue through the full 20mm? Send cut-edge photos of this exact colour. 3) Single pressed mono body, or laminated / mesh-backed / layered? 4) TDS: water absorption, flexural/MOR, Mohs. 5) Can you supply ~30 m² from stock, or is MOQ higher? 6) FOB price, packing, slab weight, container loading, ocean freight to our port. Do not quote surface-only inkjet or coloured body without vein-through.
