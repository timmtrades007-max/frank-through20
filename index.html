Vein school

What the cut edge must look like

Use these as Frank’s pass/fail. Before deposit, demand the same cut-edge photo of the exact colour + 20mm thickness.

PASS · Through-vein

Vein runs through 20mm

Pattern continues into the body. Waterfall edges and straight profiles look like stone. This is what Tim asked for.

PARTIAL · Colour body

Body tinted, vein on face

Biscuit colour matches background, but veins stop at the surface. Often sold as “full body” — not true through-vein.

FAIL · Surface print

White/plain core

Inkjet/glaze on top only. Cut edge shows blank ceramic. Reject for exposed edges unless you miter-wrap.

Can we order only 30 m² instead of 50?

Usually not from factory production. Most export MOQs are 100 m²+, 400 m², or 1 container. 30 m² ≈ 6 slabs is a sample / stock / trader move.

Possible: stock traders, Jinovation retail claim 8 m², distributor remnants Unlikely: LingBiao / Lumina / Anatolia / Laminam production runs Freight warning: LCL on 1.5 t costs far more per m² than a full container

RFQ

Copy this to every supplier

Need 1600×3200×20mm sintered stone — whole body with vein throughout thickness (through-body vein / 对位通体). Please confirm: 1) Is pigment mixed into the raw material body, or only a printed/glazed surface layer? 2) Do veins continue through the full 20mm? Send cut-edge / cross-section photos of this exact colour (like a workshop edge photo). 3) Single pressed mono body, or laminated / mesh-backed / layered? 4) TDS: water absorption (ISO 10545-3), flexural strength / MOR (ISO 10545-4), Mohs hardness. 5) Can you supply ~30 m² (about 6 slabs) from stock, or is MOQ higher? 6) FOB price, packing, slab weight, container loading qty, and estimated ocean freight to our port. Do not quote surface-only inkjet or coloured body without vein-through.
